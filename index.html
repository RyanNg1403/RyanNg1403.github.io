I like to know
how things work.

The projects change, but I keep coming back to the same questions: can it be useful, can I understand it, and can I explain it to someone else?

How do we get models
to do useful work?

Agents, MCP, memory, and evals—the practical bits that help models use tools without turning the whole system into a mystery.

What’s happening
under the hood?

Inference speed, KV-cache behavior, batching, quantization, and GPUs—the stuff that decides whether an idea works outside a notebook.

How do we make an idea
click?

Multimodal research, learning tools, explainers, and small playful products that give a technical idea somewhere to land.
